How it works

One sheet, 16 poses

The model gets your character and the 16 pose descriptions from the template and draws a 4×4 transparent sprite atlas with no text — one call keeps the character consistent across the pack.

Captions & motion in your browser

The sheet is sliced on detected gutters, each sprite gets an outlined caption, a small icon and one of 16 motion presets (nod, jump, sway, tremble…), 24 frames at 80 ms.

Export the whole pack

Download each GIF, the full ZIP (GIF folder + offline preview page + atlas), or a short montage video to share the pack.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes — 16-sticker packs are free with a small daily limit per visitor. Bigger 48 and 100-sticker packs are coming soon.

Which characters can I upload?

Only characters you created or have permission to use. Do not upload others' copyrighted or trademarked characters, or photos of real people without their consent.

Why does the AI not write the text?

Image models often misspell text. Drawing poses without text and adding captions in the browser keeps the text sharp and lets you edit it without regenerating.

Do the GIFs have a transparent background?

Yes. Each GIF uses one shared palette plus a transparent index, frame disposal “restore to background” and infinite loop, so it looks clean on light and dark chat themes.

Can I use them as WeChat stickers?

The format (320×320 animated GIF, ~2 s loop) is a common sticker size, but WeChat and other platforms review packs under their own rules. We make no claim that a pack will be approved.

How long does it take?

The AI atlas usually takes 1–3 minutes (5+ minutes when the provider queue is busy). Rendering the 16 GIFs in your browser takes a few seconds.

What model draws the poses?

Currently GPT Images 2.6 Flare (image-to-image or text-to-image, transparent PNG atlas) via TryAllAPI. Your browser then slices the atlas into captioned GIFs.
